Why it matters

Why the Right Consultants in India Make All the Difference

The Scale of the Opportunity

Every year, over 1.3 million Indian students study abroad—to build genuinely global careers, access research facilities that don't exist in India, and earn degrees that open doors everywhere. Visa policies, deadlines and scholarship requirements change every year, making experienced guidance genuinely important.

What Makes a Good Consultant

Not all study-abroad consultants are the same. Trans Globe counsellors are specialists, not generalists—each focuses on specific countries and visa processes. With 800+ university affiliations, recommendations focus on what is right for the student.

Covering All of India

Trans Globe has 16 offices across India plus an international office in Kathmandu, Nepal and online counselling. This reach helps our counsellors understand students from different cities, academic systems and backgrounds.

The Countries We Specialise In

We help students study in Australia, Canada, the USA, the UK, Germany, New Zealand, Ireland, Singapore, Dubai & the UAE, and across Europe. Our specialists continuously track destination-specific requirements and post-study opportunities.

Our Track Record

Since 1992, Trans Globe has placed more than 70,250 students at universities in over 10 countries. Its 98.7% visa success rate and strong scholarship outcomes are built on decades of experience and a clear understanding of what universities and visa officers require.

Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the questions students and families ask before beginning their international education journey.

What does a study-abroad consultant do?

A consultant helps you choose a country and course, shortlist universities, strengthen applications, find scholarships, prepare your student visa and get ready for life abroad. At Trans Globe Indore, this support is free for students.

How early should I start the application process?

For most destinations, begin 12 to 18 months before your intended intake. This leaves enough time for language tests, university applications, scholarships and visa processing.

Can Indian students get scholarships?

Yes. Universities, governments and private organisations offer awards based on merit, field of study, financial need and other criteria. Trans Globe Indore helps identify and apply for suitable options.

What is Trans Globe’s visa success rate?

The Trans Globe network reports a 98.7% visa success rate, built on decades of experience preparing complete, consistent applications for students across 10+ countries.

Can I get counselling if I cannot visit the Indore office?

Yes. Trans Globe Indore offers online counselling sessions with the same detailed, specialist guidance available at our Indore office.
